# Revagenix, Inc.

> Revagenix is a private, clinical-stage precision-therapeutics company in San Francisco,
> California, founded in 2019. It engineers purpose-built medicines for serious chronic
> disease. Its lead program, Rev-56, is a first-in-class inhaled therapy with a novel
> mechanism of action targeting chronic Pseudomonas infection in non-cystic-fibrosis
> bronchiectasis (NCFB), entering Phase 1 clinical development in 2026.

Revagenix is a therapeutics developer, not a software vendor. As of 2026-08-26 it
publishes NO public API, no SDK, no developer portal, no machine-readable contract
(OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf or WSDL), no MCP server and no A2A agent card.
Agents should treat this company as a documentation-only surface: read the pages below,
and use the contact form for any business inquiry. There is nothing here to call.
